A cinematic one-page site for Amaro.fy, a handmade crochet studio in Belo Horizonte that makes exclusive pieces to order and closes every sale over WhatsApp.

What it is

Website for Amaro.fy, a crochet studio in Belo Horizonte that makes every piece from scratch, in the client's size and color. The page tells the made-to-order story, shows the four-step process and the finished pieces with size and care guidance, and closes with an order form that writes the WhatsApp message by itself.

What it does

Scroll-scrub hero

The opening plays a 100-frame image sequence drawn to a canvas as you scroll, with a widescreen set for desktop and a vertical set for phones.

WhatsApp order builder

The order form and size chips assemble a ready-to-send WhatsApp message, so the client lands in a conversation already filled with name, piece and size.

Pinned horizontal gallery

Finished pieces scroll sideways in a pinned track on desktop and fall back to native swipe on touch, every item redoable in the client's color and size.

Knitted thread signature

A single SVG crochet thread runs down the margin and draws itself as the page is scrolled, tying the craft idea to the whole layout.
