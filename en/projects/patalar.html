What it is

Patalar is a fictional pet shop made for this study, combining an online store with a service center.

It's for dog and cat owners who want to shop and care for their pet in one place. The main action is buying; the second is booking a service.

The hero "Tudo o que seu pet precisa para uma vida mais feliz e saudável." opens with a dog and cat on an orange sofa, followed by the origin story in "Começou com um cão sem raça", the "coleção premium" by category and "Banho, saúde e hospedagem no mesmo lugar". Orange and the Fraunces serif keep it warm.

What was designed in every detail.

Orange and a warm serif

The orange comes from the sofa in the main photo and returns on the house-brand packaging. Fraunces headlines make it feel like a home, not a shelf.

Guarantees up front

A strip under the hero covers same-day delivery, receipts, Pix and support. It settles the worries of someone buying pet food in a hurry.

Services with a book button

Grooming, vet, boarding and group walks each get a photo card and a booking link. The store doubles as the pet's calendar.

An origin story with video

The story section shows the founding couple with their dog and a play button. It says the brand started with a mutt, which builds connection.
