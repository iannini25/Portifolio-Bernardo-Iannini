What it is

broto.

is a fictional cold-pressed green juice brand made for this study, with bottles pressed in the morning and delivered the same day. It's for people who want a healthy habit without fuss. The site needs to get them ordering single bottles or the weekly kit.

After "UMA ESCOLHA MAIS VERDE PARA O SEU DIA A DIA", the page shows ingredients in "MANTENHA VERDE. MANTENHA SIMPLES", a three-step process in "SIMPLES POR NATUREZA", flavors in "ESCOLHA O SEU VERDE" and a zip code lookup. Pistachio green in rounded blocks feels like a bottle label.

What was designed in every detail.

Pistachio green in blocks

Each section is a rounded block alternating cream and pistachio. Heavy Archivo headlines read like a product label.

A three-step process

Pick, cold-press and deliver chilled sit on numbered, staggered cards. It justifies the price without mentioning it.

The weekly kit up front

Next to single flavors, a dark green card sums up the six-bottle kit and total. That's the option the brand wants to sell.

Zip code before ordering

The page ends by asking for a zip code to show delivery times. It answers whether the brand reaches your street.
