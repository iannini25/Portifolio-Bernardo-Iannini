01/What it is

What it is

Ventara is a fictional dealer of sports cars and grand tourers, created for this study. It targets people who want a special car without a drawn-out buying process. The action that matters is booking a test drive.

The page opens with "FEITO PARA DESAFIAR LIMITES" in huge condensed type over the Éter GT, lists stock under "Encontre seu próximo carro", explains the service in "Mais do que só dirigir" and gives "FEITO PARA PERFORMAR" a dark band with clickable hotspots on the car. Alternating light and black bands give it the pacing of a car magazine.

Visual signatureThe giant Anton headline set right against a white sports car, with a spec card and a 360° view button.

04/Decisions

What was designed in every detail.

01

Huge Anton over cool photos

The condensed headline fills half the width while photos lean grey and steel blue. It reads like the cover of a car magazine.

02

Stock in short cards

Each car shows its name, category and an available or test drive tag. A final card tells how many vehicles are in the collection.

03

Specs in the hero

0 to 100 time and horsepower sit in the first fold next to the 360° view. Car people get the numbers before the copy.

04

Hotspots along the side

On the dark band, markers on the car open notes on wheels, brakes and suspension. It shows engineering without a spec sheet.
