What it is

Brisa is a fictional brand made for this study: a mental health app combining a guided journal, mood tracking and sessions with licensed psychologists.

It speaks to adults who keep putting off looking after their heads because it feels like a hassle. The site has one job: get them to download the app.

It opens on "Um canto calmo para cuidar da sua mente" against a cloudy sky, moves through privacy badges, shows the product in "Ferramentas que acompanham o seu ritmo" and closes with "Seu apoio, onde você estiver" around a phone. Sage green and open sky keep it far from a clinic look.

What was designed in every detail.

Sky and sage instead of white

The hero and footer use clouds and an open meadow instead of clinical white. Sage green is kept for buttons and badges so the tone stays quiet.

Badges that answer the fear

Right after the hero, four laurel badges cover privacy, licensed therapists and encrypted data. That's the first worry of anyone about to write down their feelings.

The app shown as tiles

A green bento shows monthly sessions, a mood calendar, journaling streaks and a booked session. Visitors get the product without reading a paragraph.

A testimonial with room to breathe

A carousel of illustrative quotes pairs large type with a close-up portrait. The footer states the app doesn't replace care and points to a crisis line.
