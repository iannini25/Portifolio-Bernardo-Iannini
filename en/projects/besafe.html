BeSafeHuman-Factor Security

Human-factor security platform: companies send simulated phishing through the channels they already use, and whoever clicks learns on the spot.

Visit the websitebesafebr.com (opens in a new tab)

Project built by Bernardo Iannini

What it is

BeSafe is a human-factor security platform for companies. The admin imports the team, sends simulated phishing through email, Outlook, Slack, Teams and WhatsApp, and picks the date. Whoever clicks immediately sees a screen showing what could have leaked and goes straight to a short video lesson on that exact scam.

The results turn into metrics in two dashboards, one for the admin and one for each employee, and an education platform keeps video modules on old and new scams, always without blaming the person.

The problem

Firewalls and antivirus protect machines, but most incidents start with a person: someone clicks a fake link, approves a payment on the phone or shares access under pressure. Companies rarely know who on their team would fall for a scam, and generic yearly trainings do not change behavior.

The hard part is testing people for real without exposing or blaming anyone, and turning each mistake into learning right at the moment it happens.

What it does

Phishing in every channel

The company sends realistic fake scams through the channels the team already uses, email, Outlook, Slack, Teams and WhatsApp, to see who falls for them.

Learning at the moment of the click

Whoever clicks sees what could have leaked and goes straight into a short video lesson about that exact scam, without blame.

Education platform

Video modules on old and new scams, informal and up to date, included in the plan for the whole team.

Human risk becomes a number

Admin and employee dashboards turn behavior into measurable risk, and an in-app assistant helps check whether a message looks suspicious.
