Moeda NobreDigital Benefits Currency

Digital benefits currency that keeps money flowing between companies, their employees and the local economy around them.

Visit the websitemoedanobre.com (opens in a new tab) See the apps

Project built by Bernardo Iannini

What it is

Moeda Nobre is a digital benefits currency. A company or a city loads people's balances, and they spend them at neighborhood shops by paying with their phone, using a QR Code and a PIN. The shop gets paid instantly and cashes out by PIX whenever it wants.

In practice it is three products talking to each other: the web platform, where companies, shops and the operations team track and approve everything, and two apps, one for people who receive the benefit and one for the shops that sell.

The problem

Traditional corporate benefits are a voucher that only works at the big chains. The money a company puts in as a benefit leaks straight out of the economy around the employee. The local shops, where people actually live and spend, are left out because they have no simple way to accept that voucher or to get paid for it.

Moeda Nobre flips that around. But the real challenge was never just "build a wallet." It was moving real money between four players (the platform, the company, the merchant and the employee) with approval, segregation of duties and auditing, so nothing ever moves without leaving a trace.

Two apps, one at each end

The people who receive the benefit and the shops that sell use different apps, each built for its own job. Both are live in the stores.

Moeda Nobre Benefícios

For people who receive the benefit

The company or city benefits on the phone. People see how much they can spend and pay at accredited shops by scanning a QR Code and confirming with a PIN, no card needed.

  • Balance of each benefit at any time
  • Pay in three taps: QR Code, benefit and PIN
  • Statement of where every real went
  • Map of the shops that accept it

Moeda Nobre Vendas

For accredited shops

The merchant app: type the amount, show the QR Code on the phone and the sale lands instantly, with no card machine and no paper. Each seller has their own access and every sale is logged.

  • Charge in three taps: amount, QR Code, done
  • Instant confirmation, no customer screenshot needed
  • Sales history with receipts
  • Works on any phone, no card machine

How it works

  3. 03 Employee pays QR Code + PIN
  4. 04 Merchant cashes out PIX or TED

What it does

A currency of its own

Contracting companies deposit funds on the platform and hand them to employees as monthly balances they can spend nearby.

Spent at partner merchants

Employees pay by scanning a QR Code and confirming with a PIN, at a network of partner merchants who pile up balances and cash out via PIX or TED.

See details
  • The merchant generates a QR with an amount and an expiry; the employee scans it and confirms with a PIN.
  • Before approving, the system checks the benefit rules (state, city, ZIP, segment) and the available balance.
  • Consumption is FIFO, oldest balances first, and a single payment can combine several benefits.
  • On withdrawal the merchant asks for an exact amount and the system consumes the stacks in FIFO order, with a proportional fee.

Four dedicated profiles

Platform operators, companies, merchants and employees each get their own dashboard, granular permissions and dedicated flows.

Built-in financial controls

Multi-step approval, segregation of duties, batch import of employees, full audit logs and LGPD compliance.

See details
  • A balance entry moves through 8 states, from draft to distribution, and can be rejected at any step with a reason.
  • Segregation of duties is strict: whoever creates doesn't approve, and whoever approves doesn't pay (403 if they try).
  • Batch import validates the whole spreadsheet before writing a single employee.
  • Everything is soft delete and every action turns into an audit record with before/after state, IP and user agent.

Result

Moeda Nobre is a B2B2C financial platform in production, not a pilot. Real money flows from the company all the way to the local shop, passing through bank-grade controls: approval across several hands, a clear split between who approves and who pays, universal soft delete and an audit record for every action. Four different profiles run the same system, each with its own panel and its own permissions.
