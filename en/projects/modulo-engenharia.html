Módulo EngenhariaHeavy-Industry Automation Site

Institutional site for an engineering firm that automates and maintains the critical machinery of heavy industry: mining, steel, offshore and energy.

Visit the websitemodulo-engenharia.vercel.app (opens in a new tab)

Project built by Bernardo Iannini

What it is

Módulo Engenharia sells automation, electrical engineering and field maintenance for industry that cannot afford to stop. The site is a single-page journey that opens on a cinematic offshore hero, walks through six services and the four operating segments, and closes on a contact block that turns a visitor into a direct WhatsApp conversation with an engineer.

What it does

Cinematic offshore hero

It degrades to a static poster under reduced-motion, no-JS or missing frames.

Four heavy-industry segments

Mining, steel, offshore and energy each get their own read, from yard machines and long-distance conveyors to blast-furnace instrumentation and offshore control and safety systems.

WhatsApp message builder

The contact block keeps WhatsApp as the primary channel: a short form assembles a ready message with name, company and segment and opens a direct conversation with an engineer, with no server round trip.
