What it is

What it is

Calmaria is a fictional natural skincare brand, built as an online store study.

It's for people who want to start caring for their skin without getting lost in actives and steps. The site has to get them to build a routine and add it to the cart.

The path goes through where-to-start categories, best sellers, a five-minute ritual kit, ingredients and a three-step routine, ending in the FAQ. Moss green with condensed Anton and a cat in the hero pull the brand away from skincare clichés.

Visual signatureAn orange Persian cat with cucumber slices over its eyes, cut out between the words NOVO SKINCARE in moss green.

Decisions

What was designed in every detail.

01

Moss green with a sense of humor

Condensed Anton in moss green and a cat in a cucumber mask break the niche's seriousness. The brand feels close without losing care.

02

Start from the need

Categories come as photo cards: moisturizers, serums, body and kits. Beginners pick where to start, not which active.

03

A routine in three steps

Cleanse, treat and moisturize, with morning and night tabs and a build-my-routine button. The sequence turns into a cart in one click.

04

Labels and doubts, straight

Ingredients like cucumber, aloe and niacinamide get their own cards, and the FAQ covers sensitive skin, pregnancy and returns. Online-buyer objections are answered.
