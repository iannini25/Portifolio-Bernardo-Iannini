What it is

Cardume is a fictional project management product made for this study, aimed at small and mid-size teams still running work on spreadsheets and group chats.

The site wants one thing: the team lead starting a free trial.

After "GESTÃO TUDO EM UM DE PROJETOS PARA TIMES MODERNOS", the page shows the dashboard, makes its case in "POR QUE TIMES ESCOLHEM A CARDUME", lists features in "Tudo o que o seu time precisa" and lays out pricing in "Comece pequeno, cresça sem trocar de ferramenta". Lime green on black and white makes it obvious where to click.

What was designed in every detail.

Rock, laptop and a note

The hero pairs a real rock, the product dashboard and a handwritten Caveat note. It dodges the usual floating mockup of software sites.

Lime green as a signal

Black, white and a single lime green. The green shows up on buttons, the recommended plan and the last word of headlines.

Plans side by side

Three plans with a monthly and yearly toggle, the middle one in black. Teams see right away they can start free and grow.

Social proof in numbers

Fictional company logos and a dark block with active teams and ratings help win over people who've never heard of the brand.
