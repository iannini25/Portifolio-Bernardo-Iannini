What it is

Filigrana is a fictional jeweler working in handmade 18k gold, created as a brand study.

It focuses on brides and families looking for wedding pieces, from earrings to commissioned necklaces. The site has to get them to book a private fitting at the workshop.

The page moves through the collections, a piece of the season with full specs and price, the workshop process and a pre-fitting FAQ. Italic Cormorant Garamond, gold and a cream background give it the tone of a wedding invitation.

What was designed in every detail.

Invitation italics and gold-orange

Every headline pairs a Cormorant italic with a spaced uppercase word. Gold-orange appears only in buttons, like a detail on a piece of jewelry.

Season piece with full specs

The featured necklace lists metal, stones, weight, lead time and price, with thumbnails to switch photos. It's a product page inside the landing.

A three-step bench process

Joint design, casting and filigree, then setting and polish are explained beside a photo of the goldsmith's hands. The weeks of waiting gain a reason.

Fittings booked by event date

The final form asks for name, WhatsApp and the wedding date. Staff know the deadline before they even reply.
