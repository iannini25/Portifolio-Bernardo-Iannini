What it is

Arco Estúdio is a fictional agency built as a design study.

It talks to business owners and marketing teams who want strategy, identity and paid media handled by one crew. The site needs to end in a project email.

The solutions section lists services as a numbered accordion, "Our latest projects" shows fictional cases like Leite Lunar, and "Got a project? Let's talk" closes on a midnight-blue band. The brand's indigo violet fills entire blocks, giving the site a poster feel.

Visual signature"ESTÚDIO CRIATIVO" in heavy type with a small pill-shaped image between the words, above a thin "BRANDING & DIGITAL".

What was designed in every detail.

Services as an index

Strategy, identity, marketing, paid media and analytics become a numbered list that opens on click. Clients find what they need without a wall of text.

Cases with the product on show

Packaging and identities appear in cards named after fictional clients. The work speaks before the description.

Two layers of proof

Photo testimonials sit next to a strip of client logos. Each backs up the other.

Indigo as a full block

Indigo violet isn't an accent here: it fills whole sections, alternating with midnight blue and white. Karla keeps it friendly and precise.
