01/What it is

What it is

This is the site you are looking at: my portfolio, where I bring together who I am, what I do, the projects I have built and a blog with behind-the-scenes posts and events. It is entirely handmade, with its own visual system, lightweight animations and instant switching between Portuguese and English.

It is also my lab: every page is built to open fast on any device and to be found by anyone searching for my name.

02/What it does

What it does for the people using it.

01

One visual language, end to end

Dark background, a single lime accent and one type family across home, blog and project pages, so everything reads as the work of the same hand.

02

Portuguese and English, one click

Every string on the site swaps language instantly, and each project and post also has its own address in each language for whoever arrives from search.

03

A page of its own for every project

Each project is told as a case: what it is, the problem, how it works, screens you can browse and who built it. Over sixty of them, each with its own accent color.

04

Made to be read by everyone

Keyboard navigation, screen reader labels and respect for reduced motion. Animation adds to the page but is never needed to understand it.
