What it is

Alcova is a concept smart home app under a fictional brand, made for this study and shown across three panels.

Its imagined user wants to see what's happening in each room and adjust light, sound and cleaning without switching screens. The main action is controlling the rooms.

The first panel opens on the live "Sala de estar" camera and the "Cômodos" list. Below sit cards for the "Quarto" robot vacuum, the "Sala de estar" pendant lamp and the "Caixas de som" player. Everything floats in frosted glass over a blurred shot of the home itself.

What was designed in every detail.

Frosted glass over the home

Cards are translucent over a blurred image of the room. The app feels like a layer on top of the house, not a separate screen.

Climate pills on the camera

Temperature, humidity, power use and air quality sit as pills over the live feed. One glance shows image and conditions together.

Each device its own scene

The robot vacuum shows battery and area, the pendant has a brightness control and the speakers have cover art and a player. Each card speaks for its object.

White, black and sea green

The palette is nearly neutral, with one black card for rhythm. Pale sea green is kept for small signals like devices online.
