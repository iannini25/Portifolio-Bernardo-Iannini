What Google I/O Extended taught me about AI

At Google I/O Extended, a talk by Ahirton Lopes on AGI and intelligent agents showed me the difference between using AI and communicating with it.

Bernardo Iannini Full Stack Developer & AI Designer

Last Saturday, I attended the Google I/O Extended, hosted by Google Developer Groups (GDG) and the highlight was the talk by Ahirton Lopes, Ph.D. — a Google Developer Expert in AI, MBA lecturer at FIAP and specialist in Machine Learning.

He talked about the progress of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and how we can use intelligent agents — systems that can work on their own to complete tasks based on specific instructions. What really stood out for me was learning the difference between just using AI and knowing how to communicate with it properly.

Many people write a basic prompt and hope for great results. But there’s a big difference between regular users and those who really understand how AI works — how it reads information, builds language, and turns it into useful answers. I learned how being clear, intentional, and having some technical knowledge can make a huge difference in the results you get from AI.

Ahirton also showed how, nowadays, AI helps us in messages, phone calls and virtual assistants — and we often don’t even notice there isn’t a real person behind it.

In fact, this post was structured by AI, using the content I learned from the event and written with a carefully created prompt.

Durante a palestra, ele abordou o avanço da Inteligência Artificial Geral (AGI) e a utilização de agentes inteligentes — sistemas capazes de agir de forma autônoma para executar tarefas com precisão, a partir de comandos específicos. Mas o que realmente me marcou foi ter entendido a diferença entre simplesmente usar uma IA e saber se comunicar com ela de forma eficiente.

Muita gente escreve um prompt básico e espera mágica. Mas existe um abismo entre o usuário comum e quem realmente entende como a IA processa informações, estrutura linguagem e transforma dados em soluções. Ele me ajudou a entender como a clareza, a intenção e o conhecimento técnico ao formular um prompt podem mudar completamente o resultado obtido.

Ahirton também mostrou como, hoje em dia, somos atendidos por IA em mensagens, ligações e assistentes virtuais com tanta naturalidade que nem sempre percebemos que não há um humano do outro lado.
