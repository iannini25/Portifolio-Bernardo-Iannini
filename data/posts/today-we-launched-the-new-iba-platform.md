Today, we launched the new IBA platform. 🌍

This project means a lot to me. I built the platform from the ground up, from the website and digital presence to the internal platform and its core experiences.

What started with a few conversations in April has now become a platform connecting people, companies and opportunities across fintech ecosystems in Brazil, Ireland, the UK and beyond.

I’m incredibly grateful to Igor Marchetti and the entire Innovation Business Alliance (IBA) for the trust, the opportunity and for having me as part of this journey.

Seeing the platform live today, presented to a selected group of leaders and professionals from different countries, was a very special moment.

A new chapter for IBA starts here: [https://ibanetwork.org/](https://ibanetwork.org/)

Thank you to everyone who made this possible. 🇧🇷 🇮🇪 🇬🇧
